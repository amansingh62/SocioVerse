# Edit Profile

## Questions this document answers

* How do I edit my profile on Socioverse?
* How can I change my username on Socioverse?
* How do I update my profile picture?
* How do I change my bio?
* Where can I edit my profile information?
* How do I update my profile details?
* How do I change my display picture or DP?
* How can I modify my profile settings?
* Where can I see my posts and saved posts?
* How do I access my profile from the menu?

## Answer

To edit your profile on Socioverse:

1. Go to your **Profile page** in the **menu on the left side**.
2. Click **Edit Profile**.
3. Update any of the following:

   * **Username**
   * **Profile picture (DP)**
   * **Bio**
4. Click **Save** to apply the changes.

Your profile information will be updated after saving.

## Viewing Posts and Saved Posts

On the **Profile page**, you can also view:

* **Your Posts**
* **Saved Posts**

## Notes

* Profile changes only affect your own account.
* Updated information will appear immediately after saving.
