* How can I see posts from people I follow?

* How do I see all posts on Socioverse?
* What does the Explore section show?
* How do I switch between Following and Explore?
* Why am I only seeing posts from people I follow?
* How can I see posts from everyone on the platform?
* Where can I browse all posts?
* What is the difference between Following and Explore?

## Answer

On the **Home page**, you can view posts in two different sections.

### Following Section

The **Following** section shows posts from:

* Users you are **following**
* **Your own posts**

This section displays content from accounts you follow so you can easily keep up with them.

### Explore Section

Next to the Following section is the **Explore** section.

The **Explore** section shows:

* Posts from **all users on the platform**
* Including your own posts and posts from other users

This allows you to discover content from everyone on Socioverse.

## How to Switch Sections

1. Go to the **Home page**.
2. Select **Following** to see posts from users you follow.
3. Select **Explore** (right beside Following) to see posts from all users.

## Notes

* The Following feed is personalized based on the accounts you follow.
* The Explore feed shows posts from across the platform.
