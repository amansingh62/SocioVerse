# Trending Hashtags

## Questions this document answers

* How can I see trending hashtags on Socioverse?
* Where can I find trending hashtags?
* How do I view posts related to a hashtag?
* What happens when I click on a trending hashtag?
* Why are trending hashtags not working in Explore?
* How can I browse posts from a specific hashtag?
* Where is the trending hashtag section located?
* Why can't I open hashtag posts in the Explore section?

## Answer

Socioverse shows the **top 5 trending hashtags** in the **Trending Hashtags section**.

To view posts related to a hashtag:

1. Go to the **Home page**.
2. Make sure you are in the **Following section**.
3. Locate the **Trending Hashtags section**.
4. Click on any **hashtag**.

You will then see **only the posts that contain that specific hashtag**.

## Important Rule

* The **Trending Hashtags feature works only in the Following section** of the Home page.
* It **does not work in the Explore section**.

## Notes

* The platform displays the **top 5 trending hashtags**.
* Clicking a hashtag filters the feed to show posts related to that hashtag only.
