# Notifications

## Questions this document answers

* How can I see my notifications on Socioverse?
* Where do I check who liked my post?
* How can I see who commented on my post?
* How do I see who followed me?
* Where can I check my recent activity?
* How do I open the notifications page?
* Where are notifications located on the platform?
* How do I see interactions on my posts?
* How can I check new followers?

## Answer

To view your notifications on Socioverse:

1. Open the **menu on the left side** of the page.
2. Click on the **Notifications** option.
3. The notifications page will display your recent activity.

## What Notifications Show

The notifications page can show activities such as:

* Users who **liked your post**
* Users who **commented on your post**
* Users who **followed you**

## Notes

* Notifications help you stay updated with activity related to your account.
* New likes, comments, and followers will appear on the notifications page.
