# View Other User Profile

## Questions this document answers

* How can I view someone else's profile on Socioverse?
* How do I open a user's profile from a post?
* How can I visit another user's profile?
* How do I see posts from a specific user?
* How can I follow someone from their profile?
* How do I check someone's posts on Socioverse?
* How do I open a profile by clicking the username?
* How can I see another user's profile page?
* Where can I follow a user?
* How do I view posts from another user?

## Answer

To view another user's profile on Socioverse:

1. Find a post created by the user.
2. Click on the **username** shown on that post.
3. You will be taken to that user's **profile page**.

From the profile page you can:

* **Follow the user**
* **View their posts**

## Following a User

On the user's profile page, click the **Follow** button to start following them.

## Viewing User Posts

All posts created by that user will appear on their **profile page**, allowing you to browse their content.

## Notes

* You can view any public user's profile by clicking their username.
* The profile page shows posts created by that specific user.
