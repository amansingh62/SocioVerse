# Delete Post

## Questions this document answers

* How do I delete a post on Socioverse?
* How can I remove a post I created?
* What steps should I follow to delete my post?
* How do I remove something I posted?
* Can I delete a post from my profile?
* Where is the delete button for a post?
* How can I remove my uploaded post?
* How do I permanently delete my post?
* Why can't I delete someone else's post?
* How do I delete a post from the feed?

## Answer

To delete a post on Socioverse:

1. Find the post you want to remove.
2. Click the **Delete** button on that post.
3. Confirm the deletion if prompted.

The post will be removed from the platform.

## Rules

* You can **only delete posts that you created**.
* You **cannot delete posts created by other users**.

## Notes

* Once deleted, the post will no longer appear in feeds or profiles.
* Deleting a post removes its content permanently.
